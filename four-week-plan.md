# Four-Week Plan for the Microsoft Interview

Four weeks at 8 to 10 hours per week is enough for an engineer who already writes code daily. The plan spends two weeks on coding, one on design, and one on stories and full practice loops. Each week ends with a finish condition, which is a test you can pass or fail.

## The plan at a glance

| Week | Focus | Hours | Finish condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coding patterns and the timed test | 8 to 10 | Two medium problems solved in 60 minutes, all your own test cases passing |
| 2 | Harder coding and class design | 8 to 10 | One hard problem explained aloud, and one class design written in 45 minutes |
| 3 | System design | 8 to 10 | One full design spoken in 45 minutes, with security and cost covered |
| 4 | Stories, the AA, and mock loops | 8 to 10 | Six stories written, and one full mock loop finished |

Candidates below level 63 can move three hours from week 3 to week 2. Candidates at 63 and above can do the opposite. See [levels](levels.md).

## Week 1: coding patterns and the timed test

- Solve the two easy and six medium problems in the Microsoft course. Start with [Buddy Strings](https://www.designgurus.io/course-play/grokking-microsoft-coding-interview/doc/buddy-strings-easy?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=microsoft-interview-guide&utm_content=four-week-plan).
- Use a plain editor and a timer. Allow 30 minutes per problem.
- Before each run, write four test cases: empty input, one element, duplicates, and the largest allowed value.
- Start an error log. Record each failed problem and the reason.

**Finish condition:** you solve two unseen medium problems in 60 minutes, and every test case you wrote passes.

## Week 2: harder coding and class design

- Solve the four hard problems in the Microsoft course. Start with [First Missing Positive](https://www.designgurus.io/course-play/grokking-microsoft-coding-interview/doc/first-missing-positive-hard?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=microsoft-interview-guide&utm_content=four-week-plan).
- Add the nine linked problems in the second table of the [question bank](question-bank.md).
- For every problem, state the plan and the time and space cost aloud before typing.
- Write classes for one small system, such as a [parking lot](https://www.designgurus.io/course-play/grokking-the-object-oriented-design-interview/doc/design-a-parking-lot?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=microsoft-interview-guide&utm_content=four-week-plan).

**Finish condition:** you explain one hard problem aloud from plan to tested code, and you write one class design in 45 minutes.

## Week 3: system design

- Learn one answer order: requirements, estimate, interface, data model, high-level design, deep dives, failures.
- Practice three designs tied to Microsoft products: chat, file sync, and a notification service.
- For each design, add three items without a reminder: security, separation of customer data, and cost.
- Answer two follow-up questions per design: ten times the traffic, and what fails first.

The lesson [Designing Dropbox](https://www.designgurus.io/course-play/grokking-the-system-design-interview/doc/designing-dropbox?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=microsoft-interview-guide&utm_content=four-week-plan) covers file sync. The [system design interview](system-design-interview.md) page lists a lesson for each product area.

**Finish condition:** you speak one full design in 45 minutes to another person, and that person can repeat your main trade-off.

## Week 4: stories, the AA, and mock loops

- Write six stories, including two real failures. End each one with the behavior you changed.
- Prepare three deep project stories for the final senior interview: one build, one failure, and one disagreement.
- Write a two-minute answer to "Why Microsoft, and why this team?"
- Run one mock loop: two coding interviews, one design interview, and one behavioral interview.
- Review the error log from week 1. Solve again every problem on it.
- Ask the recruiter three questions: the level number, the kind of design round, and whether there is a final senior interview.

**Finish condition:** all six stories are written and timed at about two minutes, and one mock loop is finished with written feedback.
